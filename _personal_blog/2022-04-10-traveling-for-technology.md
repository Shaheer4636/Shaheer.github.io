Travel is not just a break from work. It is a different kind of engineering problem. Every city has its own rhythm, its own network habits, and its own expectations about how systems should behave.

Moving through airports, working from cafes, and listening to local teams taught me how much the same platform can feel very different depending on the people and the context.
