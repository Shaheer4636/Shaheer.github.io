Remote work gave me a clearer view of how much attention depends on environment. A home office can be calm, but it can also become full of distractions if there is no structure around the day.

I learned that focus is not just a personal trait. It is shaped by routines, boundaries, and the way a day is planned. The more intentional the structure, the easier it becomes to do meaningful work without feeling pulled in every direction.

That period taught me to value depth over motion. It is much easier to stay productive when your environment supports your attention rather than constantly competing with it.
